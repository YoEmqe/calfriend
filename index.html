Features

Calorie & macro tracking

Track calories and macros by meal: breakfast, lunch, dinner, and snacks. See your daily burn vs. intake and hit your targets with the at-a-glance dashboard.

Activity

Log walking, running, cycling, and more. Track calories burned and keep your activity in sync with your daily goals.

Daily water goal

Track intake by glasses, set a daily target, turn on reminders, and view your last 7 days at a glance.

Intermittent fasting

Follow 12:12, 14:10, 16:8, or 17:7 plans. See your fasting timer, body state, and keep the fasting card on your home screen.

Profile & progress

Manage weight goal, BMI, weight history, and meal calorie targets. Stay on track with a clear view of your progress.

Meals & food

Search foods, add items to meals, and create custom foods with nutrition per 100g. Use favorites and often-used items for quick logging.

Apple Health

Connect Apple Health to sync steps, active energy, and heart rate. See today’s stats and steps over time in one place.
